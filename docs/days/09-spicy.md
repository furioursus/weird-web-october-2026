# Day 9's Scoville Table

Day 9 (Spicy) is the Scoville Table: eleven pixel-art peppers on butcher paper, beside a table of their Scoville ranges. Biting a pepper adds its heat to a running total, and the heading, the paper and the table wobble harder the more you've eaten, until you restock. Tybalt hides behind one of the peppers. It's set in Silkscreen.

- **The art comes from my Photoshop file,** `design/day-9/Peppers.psd` (`.git/info/exclude` keeps it out of git). The peppers are dithered with DitherTone Pro on a 4px grid. `npm run peppers:psd` reads the file and lists what it found; `-- --write` saves everything to `src/assets/09/`, and `-- --psd ~/Desktop/Peppers/Peppers.psd` reads a different copy. It needs [uv](https://docs.astral.sh/uv/).
  - **Layers:** "Butcher paper" becomes `paper.jpg`. It's a smooth texture, not pixel art, so it's a JPEG. Every other visible group is a pepper, named after the group ("Jalapeno" becomes `jalapeno`). `ALIASES` in the script maps "Ghost Reaper" to `ghost-pepper`; renaming the group in the PSD does the same. Hidden layers and layers named "Reference…" are skipped.
  - **Output:** each pepper is saved full size as `<id>.png` and `<id>-bitten.png`, and `peppers.json` records the canvas (1280 × 960) and, for each pepper, its art box (shadow included), the box of the pepper itself before and after the bite, and Tybalt's hiding spot. The script's composite matches Photoshop's own preview pixel for pixel.
  - **Each pepper's 4px grid starts at its own offset,** so its shadow and bite are worked out on that pepper's grid, one cell per art pixel, then scaled back up to match it exactly.
  - **Shadows** follow the reference photo, where they fall straight down: the pepper's shape smeared 3 art pixels downward and fading (`SHADOW_DROP`), blurred a little (`SHADOW_BLUR`), and ordered-dithered into two translucent browns (`SHADOW_TONES`).
  - **Bites** come from below: an arch with a notch for every tooth (`TOOTH_WIDTH`, three to six teeth), set at the height that takes half the pepper (`BITE_SHARE`), with one art pixel of pale flesh (`FLESH`) along the cut. A group named like "Fresno bitten" replaces the generated bite for that pepper.
  - **Tybalt's hiding spot** is a place where his 12 × 11 sprite (`CAT_SIZE`) sits entirely under solid pepper and entirely in the part a bite takes, as close to that part's middle as possible. The shishito is too thin, so he never hides there. If the sprite changes size, change `CAT_SIZE` to match and rerun the script.
  - **The build fails** if a pepper in `peppers.json` has no row in `KINDS`, or a row in `KINDS` has no pepper, or an image is missing.
- **The table** is `KINDS` in the page's frontmatter, mildest first, with each pepper's widely cited Scoville range; the ghost pepper's tops out at 1,041,427. Its blocks show each pepper's top end in Scoville digits, the same curve the wobble uses up to the hottest pepper.
- **The peppers are shuffled on every load** by a small inline script right after the board, which runs before the page is first drawn, so the Photoshop layout never flashes up. The biggest pepper goes first. Each one checks every spot on an 8px grid (`STEP`) where it fits 10px clear of every other pepper and shadow (`GAP`) and 16px from the paper's edge (`EDGE`), then takes the roomiest of 6 of those at random (`SPOTS_TO_COMPARE`). Picking spots purely at random missed the gaps the last small peppers need. If a layout runs out of room it starts over, up to 30 times (`MOST_RESTARTS`), then keeps the Photoshop layout. In testing, 200 layouts in a row all fit, in about 3 ms each.
- **Biting:** tap or click a pepper or its row in the table. The bitten art swaps in with a squish, the pepper rolls over 2–6° either way (`TILT_LEAST`, `TILT_MOST`), a crunch plays, and the top of its Scoville range is added to the total under the heading. A pepper can only be bitten once. Hovering or tabbing to a pepper only lights it and its row up and names it.
- **The wobble** is an SVG turbulence filter displacing the heading, the paper and the table; the buttons stay still. How hard it pushes comes from the total eaten:
  - Up to the hottest single pepper, it's measured in digits, since Scoville runs on powers of ten, and curved so a few mild ones stay gentle. That part reaches 60% of the wobble (`UP_TO_HOTTEST`). The last 40% fills in with every Scoville past that, up to the whole table added up, so eating everything melts the page harder than the ghost pepper alone. Both totals come from `KINDS`.
  - At full strength it pushes 44px (`MOST_WOBBLE`), and the ripples churn faster the hotter it gets (`BOIL_SPEED`, `BOIL_EXTRA`). It rises quickly and settles slowly (`RISE`, `SETTLE`), and it holds until a restock.
  - Measured: a jalapeño alone wobbles 10px, a habanero alone 22px, the ghost pepper alone 26px, the ghost and the habanero together 42px, the whole table 44px.
  - With reduced motion there's no wobble at all.
- **Restock** appears after the first bite. It unbites and straightens every pepper, zeroes the total, settles the wobble and hides Tybalt somewhere new; it doesn't reshuffle. Once the wobble is set to reach 55% (`REALLY_WOBBLY`, which a ghost pepper does), it becomes "Restock the table and drink some milk!", pinned in pink at the bottom of the screen above everything, 56px up so it clears the prev/next links.
- **Sounds** are in `src/assets/sounds/09/`.
  - **Bites:** `bite.mp3` to `bite-6.mp3`, cut with ffmpeg from makigai_maimai's "crunchy bite" (`makigai_maimai-crunchy-bite-450650.mp3`). The recording is eleven bites about 1.3 seconds apart; I kept the first six, from the louder first half: they peak at −7 to −9 dB, and the later ones fade to about −22 dB. Each runs from 0.02 seconds before a bite to 0.15 seconds after it, with short fades, raised to the same peak (−6 dB) and stripped of tags. A bite picks one at random, never the one just played.
  - **Spicy:** `spicy.mp3` is from Stefon on Saturday Night Live's Weekend Update, with its tags and cover art stripped. It plays once when the total reaches 8,000 SHU (`SPICY_AT`), and a restock lets it play again.
  - The files download with the page and are decoded on the first bite, so the first bite isn't silent. Volume is 0.6 (`VOLUME`). The speaker button at the top left mutes everything; it's remembered in `localStorage` (`wwo:scoville-muted`) and never wobbles.
- **The hidden cat** is Tybalt, a 12 × 11 sprite in cream with a pink nose (`TYBALT` in the frontmatter), drawn on the same 4px grid as the art. On load he hides at a random pepper's hiding spot, layered between its bitten art and its whole art, so only biting that pepper shows him. Until then he can't be clicked or tabbed to, and the "Tybalt was here." label from an earlier visit stays hidden so it can't give him away.
- **Drawing:** below 1280px wide the art is scaled smoothly, since hard-edged scaling down drops whole rows of dither; once the paper is bigger than the art, the pixels stay square.
