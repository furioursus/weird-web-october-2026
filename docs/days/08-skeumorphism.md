# Day 8's Tally

Day 8 (Skeumorphism) is Tally, a desktop printing calculator seen from above on the green felt of the old iOS Game Center. Every key does what it does on the real machine, and every result prints on a paper tape that runs up the felt; tear it off and it's crumpled onto a pile, where Dante hides. It's set in Oswald, with Courier Prime for the tape, Caveat for the sticky note and DSEG7 Classic for the display.

- **The machine lives in Illustrator:** `src/assets/08-tally.svg` (600 × 880) is the one source for its shape, and the page reads it at build time, so exporting over it updates the page. Its rectangles place everything live: `key-<action>` for each key (`key-dot` is the decimal point), `switch-power` for the power switch's track, `vfd` for the display and `printer-window` for where the tape comes out. The keys, switch, display and tape are HTML laid over the drawing at those spots, sized in container query units so they scale with it.
  - **The build fails** if a rectangle the page needs is missing, if a key rectangle has no entry in `KEYS` in the frontmatter, or if one of those rectangles is rotated or transformed. Keep them plain rectangles.
  - **Exporting:** set Object IDs to Layer Names, like day 2. Everything else in the drawing (shading, labels, the roll) is drawn as exported, with its own colors.
  - **The page's styles are global** (`<style is:global>`), because the drawing is injected from the SVG and doesn't carry Astro's scope attribute.
- **The keys** print like the real thing. + and − add to a running total and print with `+` or `−` (minus lines in red, always with the sign too); with nothing typed they repeat the last amount. ✱ prints the total with its item count and resets; #/◇ prints a typed number without adding it, or a subtotal when nothing's typed. × and ÷ print the first number and wait; += (or %) prints the second and the answer. M+, M−, ◇M and ✱M are a memory; GT prints the grand total of every ✱ total since the last GT. AVG prints the total divided by the item count, +/− flips the sign, and TAX+ and TAX− add or take off New York City's 8.875% sales tax (`TAX_RATE`). CE clears what's typed, and pressed again clears everything but memory.
  - Numbers are always two places, rounded half up. Typing stops at 7 digits before the point (`MAX_WHOLE_DIGITS`), so a total fits across the tape, and any result of 100,000,000 or more prints an error and locks the keys until CE.
  - **Results don't add themselves:** a × or ÷ answer, a percent or a tax result prints but stays out of the running total. Pressing + with nothing typed adds it.
  - **The power switch** is the only switch: off (keys just click and the display goes dark), on without printing, printing, and printing with item counts on totals (the default). It's a real range input with one stop per setting.
  - **Typing works too:** digits, `.`, `+ = - * x / %`, Enter for ✱, `s` or `#` for #/◇, `g` for GT, Backspace for the → key, Escape for CE, `t` to tear the tape, `m` to mute.
- **The tape** comes up out of the printer window, newest line at the bottom under the tinted glass, older lines rising over the roll and off the top edge of the felt (`TAPE_OVERHANG`). Each character sits a little high or low and a little light or dark, like a tired ribbon. It starts with yesterday's tape already in the machine, and Dante's paw print turns up every 13 lines (`PAW_EVERY`). It's a `role="log"`, with a hidden word after each symbol (plus, total, memory plus) for screen readers.
- **Tearing it off:** click the cutter above the window or the tape itself. The whole length crumples into one paper ball and lands on the pile: one ball in the middle, one each side, two on top, then spreading out and up through the 13 spots in `BALL_SPOTS`; past 16 balls (`MOST_BALLS`), an older one rolls away. A longer tape makes a bigger ball, from 52 to 76px across. The pile sits in the felt beside the machine when there's at least 190px of room, otherwise above it on the right.
  - **Each ball is drawn fresh:** an outer ring of 16 points makes the jagged outline, an inner ring of 8 and a middle point make the folds, and each of the 32 triangles between them is shaded by how it faces a light from the top left, plus a random crumple (`makePaperBall()`). They only tilt ±20°, so the light stays put.
- **The hidden cat** is Dante, a cutout photo of his head (`src/assets/08-dante-head.webp`, 440 × 359), passed into `HiddenCat` as its art and shown 96px wide. My full cutout lives in `design/day-8/`, which `.git/info/exclude` keeps out of git; the committed copy has no location data. He slides in right behind the 4th ball (`DANTE_AFTER`), which is always the biggest size (`HIDING_BALL_RADIUS`) so it covers his face, and sits so only the top 26% of the photo, his ears, shows over it. If a later ball lands in front of him, he rises just enough to keep his ears over that one too. Clicking the pile or his ears finds him: he rises until his chin clears the paper, chirps, and "Dante was here." shows.
  - **If the photo is re-cropped**, three numbers need updating: the ear line (`EARS_SHOWING`, a share of the photo's height), the chin line (`CHIN_FROM_BOTTOM`) and where his face sits across the photo (46.6%, the `translate` on `.pile__cat`). Scaling it needs nothing; the page reads its size from the file.
- **Sounds** are cut from "adding_machine.wav" by stephendemaria on Freesound (CC BY 3.0, credited on the felt whenever any of them are in place). I split the recording into clips with ffmpeg at every gap quieter than −45 dB lasting 0.06 seconds or more; the clips are in `design/day-8/sounds/`, with a page to listen to them. The page plays whatever is in `src/assets/sounds/08/`: `key.mp3` and `key-2.mp3` (picked at random) for the main keys, `small-key.mp3` for the five small keys, and `print.mp3` for the paper moving up a line. Any file that's missing falls back to a synthesized sound. The key clips were raised to the same peak (about −12.5 dB); the line sound stays as recorded, about 10 dB louder.
  - **The line sound is one motor:** a new line fades the last one out instead of stacking on it, unless it started under 0.25 seconds ago (`LINE_SOUND_HOLD`), so a ✱ total and its blank lines play it once.
  - **Volume** is 0.3 overall (`VOLUME`). The speaker key at the left of the small keys mutes everything; it's remembered in `localStorage` (`wwo:tally-muted`) and still works with the power off.
- **Placement:** the machine is centered on the felt, 30px low, at up to 560px wide or whatever fits the screen with 150px to spare in height.
- **Phones:** at 375px wide the five small keys come out about 17px across and the tape prints at about 7.5px, true to the real machine's proportions.
